# Training data policy
Training use is opt-in only. Users can use the service without consenting. Consent is explicit, timestamped and revocable. Deletion requests must exclude the user's content from future dataset builds and remove eligible stored data under the retention policy.

Only user-provided, self-filmed, licensed or otherwise rights-cleared material with documented permission may enter a dataset. Do not scrape advertisements, logos, trademarks or copyrighted material. Remove personal data before review and training. Dataset versions are immutable and require human approval.
