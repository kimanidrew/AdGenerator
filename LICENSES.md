# Model and dependency license register

No AI model weights are bundled or downloaded in Phase 1. No model is approved for commercial use yet.

Before adding a model, record:
- model and exact version/revision
- official model-card and license URL
- license name and commercial-use status
- attribution, redistribution and usage restrictions
- approval date and reviewer

Only open-weight models whose terms permit the intended commercial deployment may be enabled. Do not infer permission from a model being publicly downloadable.
